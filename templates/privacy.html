Privacy

Mekke is a free catalog. Browsing requires no account and stores no personal data.

Email alerts (optional)

  • What we store: your email address and your saved search criteria (brand, category, size, max price). Nothing else.
  • Why: solely to email you new listings matching your alert. Never shared, never sold, no marketing.
  • Consent: double opt-in — an alert only activates after you click the confirmation link we email you.
  • Where: stored in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Retention: unconfirmed requests are deleted automatically after 7 days. Confirmed alerts are kept until you unsubscribe.
  • Erasure: every email contains a one-click unsubscribe link that deletes your alert and your email address immediately. You can also write to {{ contact }}.

Analytics

We use Plausible, a cookieless, EU-based analytics tool. No cookies, no cross-site tracking, no personal identifiers.
